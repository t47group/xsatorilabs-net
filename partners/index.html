Venture Partnerships

Your idea deserves more than a development quote.

Traditional software development can require substantial up-front capital before a business ever reaches its first customer. Xsatori Labs offers a different path for qualified ventures: we may partner with entrepreneurs through a combination of development fees and equity participation, aligning technology development with long-term business success.

Apply to Partner How it works
Why partner

We don't just write code. We build and launch real products.

A typical development firm gets paid whether your product succeeds or not. In a venture partnership, part of our return is equity, so we only win when you do. That changes how we scope, how we build and how long we stay involved.

What you get

  • A full product team: strategy, design, engineering and launch under one roof
  • Lower up-front development cost than a traditional build
  • A technology partner invested in growth after launch, not just the hand-off
  • Experience from building and shipping our own products in gaming, fintech, social and business software

What we look for

  • A founder who knows the market and will run the business
  • A clear target customer and a real problem to solve
  • Budget to contribute toward development
  • An idea where great software is the difference-maker

FAQ

Common questions

Is every partnership part equity?

Partnerships combine development fees with equity participation. The balance depends on the scope of the build, the stage of the venture and the opportunity. We also take standard fee-based projects.

How much equity do you take?

There is no fixed number. Terms are sized to each project and agreed in a written agreement before any work starts.

What makes a venture a fit?

A founder who knows the market, a clear customer and problem, some budget to put in, and an idea where great software is the difference-maker.

Who owns the product and code?

Ownership and IP terms are part of the written agreement. In a typical partnership the product belongs to the company we're both invested in.

What happens after launch?

Because we share in the outcome, we stay involved after launch with improvements, new features and the technical side of growth, as set out in the agreement.

Venture partnerships are selective and offered at Xsatori Labs' discretion. All terms, including fees, equity and ownership, are set in a written agreement for each project. Nothing on this page is an offer to buy or sell securities.

Just need something built?

Not every project needs a partner. If you'd rather work with us on a standard fee basis, we build custom apps, platforms, AI integrations and software solutions too.
