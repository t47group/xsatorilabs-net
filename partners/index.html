Venture Partnerships

We build it. We own it with you.

For the right founders and ideas, Xsatori Labs designs, builds and launches your product in exchange for a mix of equity and cash, structured around your project, instead of a full up-front development fee.

Pitch us your idea How it works
Why partner

Skin in the game on both sides

A typical agency gets paid whether your product succeeds or not. In a venture partnership, part of our compensation is equity, so we only win when you do. That changes how we scope, how we build and how long we stick around.

What you get

  • A full product team: strategy, design, engineering and launch under one roof
  • Lower up-front cash than a traditional build
  • A partner invested in growth after launch, not just the hand-off
  • Experience from shipping our own products across gaming, fintech, social and enterprise software

What we look for

  • A founder who knows the market and will own the business
  • A clear problem and a real audience for the product
  • An idea where great software is the difference-maker
  • Some cash commitment from the founding team

FAQ

Common questions

Do you only work for equity?

No. Every partnership includes a cash component. The mix of cash and equity depends on the scope of the build, the stage of the company and the opportunity.

How much equity do you take?

There is no fixed number. We size it to the project: how much we're building, how much cash is involved, and how much ongoing work the partnership includes. Terms are agreed in writing before any work starts.

Do you still do regular paid projects?

Yes. Most of our client work is a standard paid engagement. Venture partnerships are a smaller, selective part of what we do.

What kinds of projects are a fit?

Consumer apps, marketplaces, social products, fintech and internal software where the founder brings a real market insight and the drive to grow it, and where our build plus ongoing involvement can meaningfully change the outcome.

Who owns the code?

Ownership and IP terms are part of the written agreement. In a typical partnership the product belongs to the company we're both invested in.

Venture partnerships are selective and offered at Xsatori Labs' discretion. All terms, including equity, cash and ownership, are set in a written agreement for each project. Nothing on this page is an offer to buy or sell securities.

Have an idea worth building?

Send us a short note about the idea, the market and your team. If it's a fit, we'll set up a call. Prefer a standard paid project? We do those too.
